Use caseThe problem this was built to solve expand
A ~$250M ARR B2B software company plans next year's territories, quotas, and capacity. OTE and quota are fixed by title, not derived from book size, so nothing guarantees a rep's territory can support their number. The engine standardizes quota by role, carves territories backward to a coverage target, runs a reverse waterfall to flag which books can't generate the pipeline to hit quota, and models comp cost-of-sale, then recommends the fix.
