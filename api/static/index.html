Territory & Quota Designer

Split your accounts into fair, balanced territories, set each rep's quarterly number, and check whether every rep has enough pipeline to actually hit it. Drag the sliders to try different trade-offs — the plan updates instantly. Dollar figures are monthly recurring revenue (MRR) shown in thousands ($K); quotas are per quarter. Sample data. · API
The three splits always add up to 100%. There's a real trade-off: chase perfectly even books and reps travel more; keep territories tight and books get a little less even. This tool makes that choice explicit.
